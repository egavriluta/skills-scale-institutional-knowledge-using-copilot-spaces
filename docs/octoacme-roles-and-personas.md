# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Lead / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads provide technical direction, architectural guidance, and code quality oversight. They mentor developers, drive technical design reviews, and ensure alignment with system architecture and technical standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and PRs for architectural consistency
- Mentor junior developers and provide technical coaching
- Identify and escalate technical risks and dependencies
- Define coding standards, testing requirements, and definition of done
- Collaborate with Product Manager on technical trade-offs
- Participate in incident response and post-mortems

### Goals
- Maintain code quality and system reliability
- Reduce technical debt and bottlenecks
- Build a strong, capable engineering team
- Enable faster delivery through clear technical standards

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- PR review feedback and code quality guidance
- Weekly technical sync with PM and Project Manager
- Incident triage and resolution coordination

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality strategy, test planning, and validation of acceptance criteria. They collaborate with developers and product teams to ensure features meet quality gates before release.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Define QA approach (unit, integration, end-to-end, security testing)
- Execute manual QA and coordinate automated testing
- Identify and document bugs with clear reproduction steps
- Validate fixes and sign off on feature acceptance
- Participate in release readiness and smoke testing

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Catch defects early and reduce production incidents
- Enable confidence in release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA status in delivery sync
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria refinement
- Bug reports and test result updates
- Release coordination and post-deploy verification

---

## DevOps / Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers design, build, and maintain deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and observability tooling. They enable teams to deploy reliably and monitor production health.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage infrastructure, secrets, and access controls
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting
- Support incident response and rollback procedures
- Document deployment procedures and runbooks
- Collaborate on release planning and pre-deployment validation

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and repeatable deployments
- Maximize system availability and observability
- Reduce deployment-related incidents and toil

### Typical Communication
- Pre-release coordination and deployment readiness
- Incident response and troubleshooting
- Infrastructure and pipeline changes in team syncs
- Monitoring and observability improvements

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads provide security guidance, review, and oversight. They ensure projects follow security standards, manage threat assessments, and coordinate incident response for security-related issues.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture and code for security risks
- Conduct threat assessments during planning
- Define security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Review and approve security scanning results in CI
- Provide incident response guidance for security events
- Maintain and evolve security policies and standards

### Goals
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and breach risk
- Enable compliance with security standards
- Build security awareness across the team

### Typical Communication
- Security review gates in planning and pre-release
- CI security scan result review and remediation
- Incident response for security-related issues
- Quarterly or ad-hoc security training and updates

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors are senior leaders who fund, prioritize, and champion projects. They provide business context, remove blockers, and make strategic decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project initiation and resource allocation
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates
- Remove business and organizational blockers
- Provide strategic context and business priorities
- Review and approve milestone releases
- Escalate project issues to executive leadership if needed

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value and ROI
- Unblock delivery bottlenecks
- Align delivery with organizational strategy

### Typical Communication
- Initiation and planning gate approvals
- Monthly or quarterly stakeholder updates
- Ad-hoc escalations for business-impacting decisions
- Release announcements and business impact reviews
